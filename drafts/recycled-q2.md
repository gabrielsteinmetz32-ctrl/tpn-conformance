**Subject line:** Q2 Safety Performance Index refresh

**Preview text:** Threshold changes take effect 1 July

TPN is updating the Safety Performance Index (SPI) scoring model. The revised thresholds take effect **1 July**.

TPN has adjusted the model based on the Q1 network review. The Tier 1 threshold moves from 90 to 92 and the Tier 2 threshold moves from 82 to 85. Tier 3 is unchanged.

Scores are calculated on a rolling 90 day window. The current measurement window is 1 January through 31 March.

All Partners must complete the Q2 methodology module in the Partner Learning Center (PLC) before **27 June**. The module runs 15 minutes. TPN will hold Partners at their prior tier until completion is recorded.

**Q2 completion incentive:** Partners who complete the module before 1 June will receive priority load matching consideration through the end of Q2. This incentive is available to Partners in good standing.
