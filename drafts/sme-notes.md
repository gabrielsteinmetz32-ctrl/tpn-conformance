Subject: SPI Threshold Updates + Required Training

So the SPI model has been recalibrated by the Safety Analytics team following the Q2 network review, and the revised weighting will be applied to all scores generated after 10/1. The primary change is that HOS variance and following-distance events are being weighted more heavily relative to hard-braking events, which were previously over-indexed and were producing a lot of false positives for partners operating primarily in dense metro lanes.

Scores are calculated on a rolling 90 day window. The current window for the transition is 7/1 through 9/30. It should be noted that partners will not be re-scored retroactively under the new model, so the first score reflecting the new weighting will be published on 10/15.

The threshold bands have also been adjusted. The Tier 1 band moves from 92 to 94, Tier 2 moves from 85 to 88, and Tier 3 remains unchanged at 78. Partners falling below Tier 3 will be flagged for a PSM review under the standard process outlined in Policy 6.4.

There is a mandatory training module that has been developed by PLC covering the recalibration methodology, how to read the revised scorecard in the portal, and what actions drive each of the weighted components. It runs about 20 minutes. Completion is required prior to 9/26 in order for the partner to receive their transition score. Non-completion will result in the partner being held at their prior tier until completion is recorded, which could affect load matching under the LAR prioritization logic.

Also worth mentioning that the DVIR submission requirement is unchanged, that has caused confusion in the past when we've sent SPI comms, so maybe call that out.

The module can be accessed through the Academy tab. There's a tracking report the PSMs can pull to see who has completed and who hasn't.

Let me know if you need anything else. Ideally we'd get this out early in the week so people have runway before the 26th.
