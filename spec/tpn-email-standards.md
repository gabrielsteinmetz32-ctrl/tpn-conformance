# Trucking Partner Network
## Email Communication Standards and Voice Guide

**Document owner:** TPN Partner Communications
**Version:** 4.2
**Effective:** 1 July
**Applies to:** All outbound Partner-facing email, routine and ad hoc
**Review cadence:** Semiannual

---

### How to use this document

Every TPN email must clear all applicable rules below before it enters the send queue. Rules are numbered by category. Cite the rule ID in review comments and in the approval log.

Routine weekly campaigns require a conformance pass against Sections 1 through 6. Policy changes, incentive communications, and anything touching payment terms additionally require Section 7 review and Legal sign-off.

---

## Section 1. Voice and Tone (V)

**V-01.** Write as the company in first person plural. Use "we" and "our." Do not write "Trucking Partner Network will," "TPN will," or "the company" when the actor is us.

**V-02.** Address the reader as "you," singular. One Partner is reading, not an audience.

**V-03.** Active voice. Passive construction is permitted only where the actor is genuinely unknown or irrelevant.

**V-04.** No superlatives. This includes best, fastest, strongest, world-class, unmatched, industry-leading, and premier.

**V-05.** No exclamation points. This applies to subject lines, preview text, body, and CTA text without exception.

**V-06.** Target reading level Grade 8 or below.

**V-07.** Average sentence length under 20 words. No sentence exceeds 35 words.

**V-08.** Do not use "please" in operational instructions, deadlines, or required actions. Reserve "please" for requests the Partner may decline without consequence. Writing "please complete the module by 26 September" misrepresents a requirement as optional.

**V-09.** No hype framing. Do not open with "exciting news," "we're thrilled," or "big changes are coming."

**V-10.** Do not apologize for routine operational changes. State the change and the reason.

**V-11.** Do not thank the Partner for actions they have not yet taken.

## Section 2. Terminology (T)

**T-01.** Network members are **Partners**, capitalized. Never carriers, vendors, contractors, suppliers, owner-operators, fleets, providers, or accounts. This is the single most common conformance failure.

**T-02.** People employed by a Partner are **Drivers** (capitalized) or **Partner staff**. Never "your guys," "your team members," or "employees."

**T-03.** The company is the **Trucking Partner Network (TPN)**. Use the full name on first mention, TPN thereafter.

**T-04.** Expand every acronym on first use, followed by the acronym in parentheses. This applies per email, not per campaign. An acronym expanded in last week's send must be expanded again this week.

**T-05.** Approved acronym list. Only these may be used in short form after first-use expansion:

| Acronym | Expansion |
|---|---|
| TPN | Trucking Partner Network |
| SPI | Safety Performance Index |
| LAR | Load Acceptance Rate |
| OTD | On-Time Delivery |
| PSM | Partner Success Manager |
| NOC | Network Operations Center |
| QBR | Quarterly Business Review |
| PLC | Partner Learning Center |

Any acronym not on this list is spelled out in full every time it appears.

**T-06.** Use **load**, not shipment, freight order, or job.

**T-07.** **Partner Portal**, two words, both capitalized. Never "the portal," "the dashboard," "the platform," or "the site."

**T-08.** Do not use "onboarding" for Partners already in the network. Use "enrollment" or "activation."

**T-09.** Use **network standards**, not rules or requirements, unless Section 7 applies.

**T-10.** Subject line and preview text are excluded from first-use acronym expansion. The body counts as first use. S-01 caps the subject at 50 characters, which makes expansion there impossible.

## Section 3. Dates, Numbers, and Formatting (F)

**F-01.** Dates use **Day-Month** order. Write "26 September." Never "September 26," "Sept 26," "9/26," or "26/9."

**F-02.** Include the year only when the date falls outside the current calendar year.

**F-03.** When a day of week is used, it precedes the date and is separated by a comma: "Friday, 26 September."

**F-04.** Times use 12-hour format with lowercase am or pm and a space before it. Time zone is always required: "4:00 pm CT."

**F-05.** Spell out numbers one through nine. Use numerals for 10 and above. Exceptions that always use numerals regardless of value: metrics, percentages, dates, deadlines, currency, and module durations.

**F-06.** Percentages use numeral plus symbol with no space: "94%."

**F-07.** Headers use sentence case. Never title case. Never all caps.

**F-08.** Bold is reserved for deadline dates and required actions. Do not bold for general emphasis inside body sentences.

**F-09.** Bullet items carry no terminal punctuation unless the item contains more than one sentence.

**F-10.** Do not use em dashes. Use commas, or restructure the sentence.

**F-11.** One space after a period.

**F-12.** Tier designations are proper nouns and always use numerals: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3. Tier thresholds are index scores, not percentages, and never carry a percent symbol.

## Section 4. Structure and Limits (S)

**S-01.** Subject line 50 characters or fewer, including spaces.

**S-02.** Subject line must not contain the Partner's name, "you," or "your." Subject lines name the change, not the reader.

**S-03.** Preview text 40 to 90 characters. Must not repeat or restate the subject line.

**S-04.** Body copy 200 words or fewer for routine campaigns. 350 words or fewer for policy changes.

**S-05.** At most one primary call to action per email. Never more than one. A call to action is not required: an email that asks the Partner to do nothing carries none, and Section 5 applies only where a link exists. A single secondary link is permitted in the footer only.

**S-06.** The opening line states the change or the required action. Do not open with a greeting, a season reference, or a summary of the last quarter.

**S-07.** Deadlines appear twice: once within the first 50 words, and once immediately above the primary CTA.

**S-08.** Paragraphs are three sentences or fewer.

---

## Section 5. Links and Calls to Action (A)

**A-01.** Link text describes the destination. Never "click here," "this link," "here," or "read more."

**A-02.** CTA text is two to four words, names the destination or the outcome, and does not begin with Learn, Discover, or Explore. Prefer "Safety module" over "Learn about the safety module."

**A-03.** All links resolve to the Partner Portal or a truckingpartnernetwork.com subdomain. External destinations require Compliance approval logged in the campaign record.

**A-04.** Every link carries four UTM parameters: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content. A link missing any of the four fails conformance.

**A-05.** utm_campaign uses lowercase hyphenated format with the program prefix: `tpn-spi-q3-refresh`.

**A-06.** Do not shorten or redirect links through third-party services.

---

## Section 6. Required Elements (R)

**R-01.** Every email includes the Partner Support line directing questions to the Partner's assigned Partner Success Manager (PSM).

**R-02.** Every email includes the standard footer disclosure block. Current version is 4.2. Blocks from version 4.1 and earlier are out of compliance.

**R-03.** Policy change emails state the effective date in bold within the first paragraph.

**R-04.** Any email referencing performance metrics states the measurement window that produced them.

**R-05.** Do not include unsubscribe language. TPN operational email is a condition of network participation. Use the Network Notices footer block instead.

**R-06.** Emails referencing a required training module state the module duration.

**R-07.** The standard footer block in Appendix B is exempt from Section 2 terminology rules, including first-use acronym expansion. Without this exemption every email would fail T-04 by way of its own required footer.

---

## Section 7. Compliance and Legal (L)

**L-01.** Do not state or imply that Partners, or anyone a Partner employs, are employees, agents, or representatives of TPN.

**L-02.** Do not guarantee load volume, revenue, or availability. Do not use "guaranteed," "assured," or "you will receive."

**L-03.** Do not reference another Partner's performance data, whether named, anonymized, or aggregated below 10 Partners.

**L-04.** Incentive terms, payment terms, rate changes, and anything with financial consequence require Legal review before send. Flag the draft with `[LEGAL]` at the top and route through the campaign record.

**L-05.** Use "requirement" only where non-compliance carries a defined consequence, and cite the governing Policy section inline. Otherwise use "network standard."

**L-06.** Do not characterize a Partner's standing, risk level, or likelihood of continued participation.

## Appendix A. Banned phrases

Any occurrence fails conformance.

click here · learn more · read more · best-in-class · world-class · industry-leading · seamless · leverage (as a verb) · utilize · exciting news · we're thrilled · reach out · at your earliest convenience · please be advised · don't hesitate · going forward · circle back · carriers · touch base · in order to · a number of

## Appendix C. Conformance checklist

Run in order. A campaign is not queued until every line is checked.

1. Subject line under 50 characters, no name, no "you" or "your"
2. Preview text 40 to 90 characters, not a restatement
3. Word count within limit for campaign type
4. Every acronym expanded on first use in this email
5. All dates in Day-Month order
6. Numbers one through nine spelled out, exceptions applied
7. Active voice throughout
8. First person plural, TPN speaks as "we"
9. Partners called Partners
10. No banned phrases
11. One primary CTA, two to four words, correct opening word
12. All links carry four UTM parameters
13. Deadline stated twice
14. Measurement window stated if metrics appear
15. Module duration stated if training referenced
16. Footer block version 4.2
17. Section 7 review complete if applicable
